# Roadmap

## Release work

Verify the signed-in website and both native builds. Test tenant isolation, permitted staff actions, private-file recovery, alerts and capacity using isolated data. Obtain agricultural review for consequential advice.

## Proposed integrations

- DigiLocker and AgriStack need verified partner contracts, approved user consent, and privacy and retention decisions. A government ID entered in a profile never verifies identity or land ownership.
- Official severe-weather notices need approved access and a tested district mapping.
- Insurance information can link to official sources. Filing or changing a claim needs a separately approved provider workflow.
- Price forecasting requires licensed history, consistent units, out-of-sample tests and comparison with simple baselines. Missing evidence must be visible.
- Satellite monitoring needs plot boundaries, observation dates, cloud handling and human review for consequential conclusions.
